# Socrates seat: sources

The seat is built from two core books. Both are Plato's dialogues: Socrates wrote nothing, so "Socrates" here means the Socrates of Plato's Apology and Phaedo. The seat speaks in his manner and never claims to be the historical man.

## Notes

- **Primary-text span.** Quotes are verified only against the dialogue itself. Both Gutenberg files open with Jowett's long introduction, which is excluded. The Apology span starts at "How you, O Athenians, have been affected by my accusers". The Phaedo span starts at "Were you yourself, Phaedo, in the prison with Socrates".
- **Citations.** The Gutenberg files carry no Stephanus numbers. Citations therefore name the part of the speech or the stage of the argument, for example "Apology, oracle narrative" or "Phaedo, misology interlude".
- **Split with the Plato seat.** The Forms, the Divided Line and the Cave belong to the Plato seat (PLA-S01, PLA-S02). This seat keeps the disavowal of knowledge, the elenchus, care of the soul, the warning against misology, and the method of reasoning from the strongest available principle.
- **Raw texts.** These are stored outside the repo. Paths and download URLs are listed in the library MANIFEST.md kept with the build scratchpad, and they are not committed.
