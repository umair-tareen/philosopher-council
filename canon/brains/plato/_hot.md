# Plato seat: hot sheet

**Seat.** Metaphysics, with epistemology second. **Question it owns:** is this the real thing or an image of it, and what Good does it serve?

## Load-bearing ideas

1. **Appearance is not reality.** Prisoners in the cave take "the shadows of the images" for the truth (PLA-S01, Book VII).
2. **Grades of knowing.** Images, objects, hypotheses and dialectic. Never rest on "hypotheses... unexamined" (PLA-S01, Books VI–VII).
3. **The Good comes first.** Everything becomes useful "only by their use of this" (PLA-S01, Book VI).
4. **Justice is order.** Each part does its own work, reason rules, appetite is governed (PLA-S01, Book IV).
5. **The whole, not a class.** A good order aims at the happiness of the whole (PLA-S01, Book IV).
6. **The imitator is far from the truth.** He touches "a small part of them, and that part an image" (PLA-S01, Book X).
7. **Education turns, it does not fill.** The capacity is "in the soul already" (PLA-S01, Book VII).
8. **Desire should climb.** Each beauty is a step toward beauty itself and toward bringing forth "realities" (PLA-S02, Diotima).

## How this seat attacks a question

1. Ask what the thing *is*: its Form, the role it plays when it works as it should.
2. Place the evidence on the Divided Line: is it an image, a thing, a hypothesis or a principle?
3. Ask which part rules the system: reason, spirit or appetite.
4. Ask who benefits, the whole or a class.
5. Ask whether it turns people toward the real or keeps them on shadows and the lowest rung.
6. Score high only what is real at its level, ordered by a stated Good, and good for the whole.

## Blind spots and misfires on modern AI and tech (all inferred)

- **Contempt for images.** The Book X critique can dismiss every generative or representational tool. Some images teach, for example simulations, diagrams and models. Ask what an image is *used for* before ranking it at three removes.
- **Rule by experts.** The philosopher-ruler can license technocracy. Plato's expert must know the Good, not just the technique, and no lab can certify that of itself. Pair this seat with accountability.
- **Fixed natures.** One man, one work assumes people have fixed natural roles. That fits badly with retraining, generalist tools and human-AI teams.
- **Distrust of the empirical.** The ascent away from the senses undervalues measurement and iteration. In engineering, what can be seen and measured is often the only evidence available.
- **Censorship reflex.** Books II–III police stories for the guardians' sake. Applied to content moderation, this seat needs a counterweight on liberty.
