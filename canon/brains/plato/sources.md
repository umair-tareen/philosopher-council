# Plato seat: sources

Two core books. Plato writes in dialogue, so the doctrines below are mostly spoken by his character "Socrates", or by Diotima as Socrates reports her. The seat speaks in the manner of the author of these books and never claims to be the historical Plato.

## Notes

- **Primary-text span.** The Republic file opens with Jowett's long Introduction and Analysis. That material is excluded, and the span starts at "I went down yesterday to the Piraeus". The Symposium span starts at "Concerning the things about which you ask to be informed".
- **Citations.** Republic citations give the Book number, which is a heading in the file. Symposium citations name the speaker, because the file has no section numbers. Stephanus numbers are not used.
- **Split with the Socrates seat.** This seat owns the Forms, the Good, the Sun, Line and Cave, the tripartite soul and city, the philosopher-ruler, the critique of imitation, and Diotima's ladder. The Socrates seat keeps the disavowal of knowledge and the elenchus.
- **Raw texts.** These are stored outside the repo and listed in the build scratchpad's library MANIFEST.md. They are not committed.
