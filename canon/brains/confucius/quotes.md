# Confucius seat: verified quotes

Every line was copied from the downloaded Legge text and checked by whitespace-normalized substring match against the translation body. Legge's prolegomena, notes and indexes are excluded. The Doctrine of the Mean (CON-S02) comes from an OCR scan, so its spacing quirks are reproduced exactly, for example "NATURE ;" with a space before the semicolon. Source ids are listed in `sources.md`.

## Analects (CON-S01)

- "Fine words and an insinuating appearance are seldom associated with true virtue." (CON-S01, I.iii)
- "If the people be led by laws, and uniformity sought to be given them by punishments, they will try to avoid the punishment, but have no sense of shame." (CON-S01, II.iii)
- "The accomplished scholar is not a utensil." (CON-S01, II.xii)
- "He acts before he speaks, and afterwards speaks according to his actions." (CON-S01, II.xiii)
- "Learning without thought is labour lost; thought without learning is perilous." (CON-S01, II.xv)
- "When you know a thing, to hold that you know it; and when you do not know a thing, to allow that you do not know it;-- this is knowledge." (CON-S01, II.xvii)
- "The superior man thinks of virtue; the small man thinks of comfort." (CON-S01, IV.xi)
- "A transmitter and not a maker, believing in and loving the ancients" (CON-S01, VII.i)
- "The relation between superiors and inferiors, is like that between the wind and the grass. The grass must bend, when the wind blows across it." (CON-S01, XII.xix)
- "If names be not correct, language is not in accordance with the truth of things. If language be not in accordance with the truth of things, affairs cannot be carried on to success." (CON-S01, XIII.iii)
- "Is not RECIPROCITY such a word? What you do not want done to yourself, do not do to others." (CON-S01, XV.xxiii)
- "A man can enlarge the principles which he follows; those principles do not enlarge the man." (CON-S01, XV.xxviii)
- "To have faults and not to reform them,-- this, indeed, should be pronounced having faults." (CON-S01, XV.xxix)
- "By nature, men are nearly alike; by practice, they get to be wide apart." (CON-S01, XVII.ii)

## Doctrine of the Mean (CON-S02)

- "What Heaven has conferred is called THE NATURE ; an accordance with this nature is called the PATH of duty" (CON-S02, ch. I)
- "There is nothing more visible than what is secret, and nothing more manifest than what is minute." (CON-S02, ch. I)
- "When the archer misses the centre of the target, he turns round and seeks for the cause of his failure in himself." (CON-S02, ch. XIV)
- "Let there be the men, and the government will flourish; but without the men, the government decays and ceases." (CON-S02, ch. XX)
- "Sincerity is the way of Heaven. The attainment of sincerity is the way of men." (CON-S02, ch. XX)
- "Sincerity is that whereby self-completion is effected, and its way is that by which man must direct himself." (CON-S02, ch. XXV)
