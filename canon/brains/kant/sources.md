# Kant seat: sources

Two core books: the *Groundwork* on ethics and the first *Critique* on epistemology. The seat speaks in Kant's manner and never claims to be the historical Kant.

| KAN-S02 | Kant, *The Critique of Pure Reason* | J. M. D. Meiklejohn | 1855 | Published before 1931; public domain in the US; Project Gutenberg eBook #4280 | https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/4280 |

## Notes

- **Primary-text span.**
  - KAN-S01 runs from Kant's Preface ("Ancient Greek philosophy was divided into three sciences") through the end of the Third Section.
  - KAN-S02 runs from the "PREFACE TO THE FIRST EDITION 1781" to the end of the book.
  - The Gutenberg front matter and tables of contents are excluded.
- **Citations.**
  - KAN-S01 is cited by First, Second or Third Section, Kant's own divisions as headed in the file.
  - KAN-S02 is cited by the part headings the file shows: the Prefaces, the Introduction, the Transcendental Aesthetic, the Transcendental Logic with its Analytic and Dialectic, and the Transcendental Doctrine of Method.
  - A/B pagination is not used, because the file does not carry it.
- **Italics.** Meiklejohn's emphasis appears in the file as `_word_`. Quotes keep it exactly.
- **Raw texts.** These are stored outside the repo and listed in the build scratchpad's library MANIFEST.md. They are not committed.
