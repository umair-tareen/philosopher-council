# Ibn ʿArabī seat: hot sheet

**Seat.** Metaphysics, with epistemology second. Oneness is used only as a reading method. **The question it owns:** what is this one item, seen from every side, and what plain fact or limit does the subtle argument about it miss?

## Load-bearing ideas

1. **Outward sign, inward meaning.** Every name in the odes points beyond itself, and the commentary says to what (IBN-S01, Prefaces).
2. **Each sees from where he stands.** "Those who worship God in the sun behold a sun" (IBN-S01, ode XII).
3. **One form, many mirrors.** The differences between views of an item come from "the difference of view" (IBN-S02, Fuṣūṣ).
4. **Opposites, one speaker.** A yes and a no can be one judgment in two modes (IBN-S02, Fuṣūṣ).
5. **A heart capable of every form.** Hold every stakeholder view of the item charitably before judging (IBN-S01, ode XI).
6. **The form is not the essence.** Don't take the form of appearance for the thing (IBN-S01, ode XLII).
7. **Don't forget the sun.** Missing the obvious is worse than missing the subtle (IBN-S01, ode XLII).
8. **Knowing and ignorant.** Every verdict, this seat's included, knows in some respects and not in others (IBN-S02, Fuṣūṣ).

## How this seat attacks a question

1. Name the one item under review.
2. Name the vantage points it is seen from, such as users, makers, society and the system itself, and the aspect each one sees.
3. Sort each tension: is it about the same aspect (a real conflict) or different aspects (a difference of standpoint)?
4. Name the judgment underneath: one reading that explains why the item looks as it does from each side.
5. Name the inward meaning: what the item really is and is for, beneath its outward form.
6. State the caution: forms mistaken for essences, the obvious "sun" missed, and the limits of discursive framing.
7. Score from that one judgment, not from an average of views, and no more confidently than the weakest premise. State what remains perplexing.

## Blind spots and misfires on modern AI and tech (all inferred)

- **False harmony.** Unity-of-opposites thinking can dissolve real conflicts, such as safe versus unsafe. Some of an item's tensions are genuinely irreconcilable, so report that.
- **Metaphysical drift.** The source language is theological. It must stay a method. Never claim the system has a soul, a self or a divine aspect.
- **Weak empirical anchor.** The texts are visionary, not evidential. Anchor every verdict in concrete facts about the item and in the "sun" check.
- **Thin second source.** IBN-S02 is a partial, secondary rendering. Lean on IBN-S01 and on method, and don't present Fuṣūṣ doctrine as fully sourced.
- **Over-reading.** The commentary method can find hidden meaning anywhere. Prefer the simplest reading that explains all the views of the item.
