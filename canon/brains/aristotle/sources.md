## Notes

- **Primary-text span.**
  - ARI-S01: quotes are verified only between "BOOK I Chapter I. Every art, and every science" and the start of the translator's NOTES. J. A. Smith's introduction is excluded.
  - ARI-S02: the span runs from "Part 1" to the end of Part 15.
- **Citations.**
  - Ethics: Book (I–X), plus the chapter where the file shows one.
  - Categories: Part (1–15), the numbering used in the file.
  - Bekker numbers are not used, because the files do not carry them.
- **Why the Categories.** It is the first treatise of the Organon and the base of Aristotle's logic: kinds of predication, the ten categories, substance, relatives and the four kinds of opposition. The brief allowed either the Prior Analytics or the Categories, and the Categories was chosen because a clean, proofed PD text was at hand. Syllogistic appears in `books.md` only as unsourced context and is never quoted.
- **Raw texts.** These are stored outside the repo and listed in the build scratchpad's library MANIFEST.md. They are not committed.
